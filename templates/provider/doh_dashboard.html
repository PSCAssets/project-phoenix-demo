ℹ DOH Reporting Dashboard — How This Works
What this screen does
⏱Your home base for mandatory DOH reporting. Shows all open report records across every state, organized by overdue status, state, and condition.
📊5 stat cards at the top give you an instant read: total open, overdue (SLA breached), in progress, submitted today, and this week's total.
🌏State grid highlights which states have overdue or high-volume records — red border means something needs your attention now.
Workflows this solves
🔴Previously: Previously: no centralized view of DOH reporting status. Coordinators tracked obligations in spreadsheets or email.
🟢Now: Now: every open obligation surfaces automatically the moment a lab result is finalized in Athena. Nothing falls through the cracks.
🔴Previously: Previously: no SLA visibility until a report was already late.
🟢Now: Now: overdue records are surfaced in red with a days-open counter. Business-day SLA runs automatically.
Key design decisions
⚡Records are auto-created by the DOH Rule Engine — you never manually enter a report.
📧Click any state card to jump to the queue filtered for that state.
⏸SLA counts business days only — weekends and holidays are excluded.
