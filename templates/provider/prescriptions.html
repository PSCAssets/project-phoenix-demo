Prescriptions — Page Guide
What this screen does
💊Active and recent prescriptions for all of Dr. Sarah Lee's patients. Shows medication, dose, refills remaining, pharmacy, and last fill date.
✅Refill requests from pharmacy or patient appear here with one-click approve or deny. Denials require a reason that is sent back to the pharmacy automatically.
⚖DDI (Drug-Drug Interaction) and DGI (Drug-Gene Interaction) flags surface inline on prescriptions with known interactions — flagged by the middleware integration layer.
Workflows this solves
🔴Previously: Previously: refill requests came via fax or Athena message. Providers processed them manually, with no DDI check at refill time.
🟢Now: Now: refill queue is here, DDI flags are surfaced automatically at the time of approval — not just at initial prescribing.
🔴Previously: Previously: DDI and DGI warnings existed only at initial prescribing in Athena. Refills bypassed the check.
🟢Now: Now: every refill approval runs through the DDI/DGI middleware. Providers see the flag before approving, not after.
Key design decisions
⚠DDI/DGI flags are advisory, not blocking — provider can approve with an acknowledgment note.
💊Controlled substances require a PDMP check confirmation before approval is allowed.
