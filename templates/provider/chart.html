Testosterone Replacement Therapy — Monitoring Guidelines Published
Baseline and follow-up labs for TRT should include Total Testosterone, Free Testosterone, Hematocrit (HCT), and PSA (men 40+). Recheck labs 90 days after initiation or dose adjustment, then every 6–12 months. Monitor for erythrocytosis (HCT >54%) and evaluate PSA per age-appropriate screening guidelines.
Care Product: Testosterone Care · Topic: Lab Monitoring
Trough vs. Peak Symptom Patterns — Patient Education Published
Patients on injectable TRT (e.g., Testosterone Cypionate) may notice symptom fluctuation between doses — fatigue and low energy in the days preceding the next injection (trough) versus improved energy shortly after (peak). Adjusting the injection interval (e.g., q14d → q10d) or moving to a more frequent, lower-dose schedule can reduce trough symptoms.
Care Product: Testosterone Care · Topic: Patient Education
