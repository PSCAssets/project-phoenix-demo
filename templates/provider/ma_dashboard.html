ℹ MA Dashboard — How This Works
What this screen does
📋Lupe's home view as Medical Assistant. Shows your current task queue across all active workflows — PCP follow-ups, intake tasks, patient callbacks, and pending vitals documentation.
⚖Priority and due-date chips let you triage at a glance. Overdue tasks appear at the top in amber.
📞Stat cards show: total open tasks, due today, completed today, and pending provider response.
Workflows this solves
🔴Previously: Previously: MAs worked from a mix of verbal handoffs, emails, and sticky notes with no unified task list.
🟢Now: Now: every task auto-assigned to the MA role appears in one queue — no context switching, no missed handoffs.
🔴Previously: Previously: no visibility into what was overdue until a patient or provider followed up.
🟢Now: Now: overdue tasks are highlighted with a counter and sort to the top automatically.
Key design decisions
⚡Tasks are auto-created from workflow triggers — intake completion, provider order, or scheduler action — not manually entered.
⏱SLA clock starts at task creation, not at login. Tasks track time open continuously.
