Lab Orders — Page Guide
What this screen does
📋History of all lab orders placed for your patients — open, pending results, resulted, and cancelled. Filter by patient, status, or lab partner.
⚖Result chips show: Pending (sent to lab, awaiting results), Resulted (results available in chart), Action Required (abnormal result flagged for provider review), Cancelled.
💾Click any resulted order to jump directly to the result detail in the patient's chart. No separate search required.
Workflows this solves
🔴Previously: Previously: providers had no consolidated view of pending lab orders. They checked Athena by patient, which meant an abnormal result could sit unreviewed for hours.
🟢Now: Now: all orders surface here with result status. Action Required orders sort to the top — abnormal results cannot be buried.
Key design decisions
⚡Action Required result triggers a provider notification and appears at top of this list — dual surfacing to ensure review.
📋Cancelled orders are logged with cancellation reason — required for audit trail.
