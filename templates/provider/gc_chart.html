Clinical Interpretation: This frameshift variant (c.5266dupC) in BRCA1 creates a premature stop codon at position p.Gln1756fs, resulting in a truncated, non-functional protein. This is one of the most common Ashkenazi Jewish founder mutations. The variant has been classified as Pathogenic by multiple independent labs and is associated with a substantially elevated lifetime risk of breast and ovarian cancer (HBOC syndrome). Per NCCN guidelines, enhanced surveillance and risk-reduction discussions are indicated.
