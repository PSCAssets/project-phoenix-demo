ℹ Manager Dashboard — How This Works
What this screen does
📊Team overview for clinical managers. Shows current staffing, active shift schedule, open cases needing escalation, and team SLA performance at a glance.
👥Timekeeping approval queue is surfaced here — pending timesheet edits from staff appear with original and edited times, waiting for your approval or rejection.
📋Escalation flags from RN and MA queues bubble up here when items are past SLA or require a clinical decision above their scope.
Workflows this solves
🔴Previously: Previously: managers had no real-time view of team workload or SLA status — they learned about problems reactively.
🟢Now: Now: the dashboard shows open items, overdue tasks, and timekeeping exceptions in one view updated in real time.
🔴Previously: Previously: timesheet corrections required email chains.
🟢Now: Now: staff submit edits in the app, manager approves or rejects in one click with full audit trail.
Key design decisions
⚠Escalations badge in the nav shows unreviewed items — clear these first.
⏱SLA performance shown for the current and prior week to track team trend.
📋Clicking any staff member's row opens their schedule and pending timekeeping items.
