ℹ RN Case Queue — How This Works
What this screen does
📋Full filterable list of all cases assigned to the RN. Filter by case type (async, escalation, chart review), status, or patient state.
⚖Open button on each row loads the case detail — chart data, prior notes, and the action panel (complete, escalate, route to provider).
⏱Days/hours open shown per row. Overdue items (past SLA) appear with a red counter and sort to the top by default.
Workflows this solves
🔴Previously: Previously: RNs had no structured queue — they worked from email notifications that could be missed or lost.
🟢Now: Now: all assigned cases are in a persistent queue. Cases don't disappear until explicitly closed — nothing falls through.
Key design decisions
⚠Escalation button is available on any case — adds a reason and routes immediately to supervising provider.
✅Bulk close for administrative cases (e.g., patient no-show follow-ups) to clear routine items quickly.
