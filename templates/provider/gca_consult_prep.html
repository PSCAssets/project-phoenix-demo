Workflows this solves
🔴Previously: Previously: GCAs and GCs both independently reviewed results and eligibility, sometimes catching errors only in the session itself.
🟢Now: Now: a structured checklist ensures all 4 prep areas are verified before the GC sees the case — errors caught earlier, sessions run smoother.
🔴Previously: Previously: blurb selection was done by memory or searching a separate document.
🟢Now: Now: searchable blurb library integrated into prep — one click selects and auto-checks the item.
Key design decisions
📊Progress bar at the top shows overall prep completion across all 4 sections.
✅'Mark Prep Complete' sends a notification to the GC and updates the Zendesk case — no separate follow-up needed.
