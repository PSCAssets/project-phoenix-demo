ℹ My Schedule — How This Works
What this screen does
📅Weekly availability entry for RN and MA staff. Set your shift type (Regular, On-Call, After-Hours, or Day Off) and start/end times for each day of the week.
✅Submit for Approval sends the week's schedule to your manager for review. Copy Last Week populates this week from the previous week's entries.
📊Each day shows an approval status: Approved (locked), Pending (submitted), or Unsaved (in progress).
Workflows this solves
🔴Previously: Previously: schedule requests were handled via email or verbal agreement with no structured visibility for managers.
🟢Now: Now: staff submit availability in a structured format. Managers see all submissions in the Team Schedule view.
Key design decisions
⏱Day Off selection disables start/end time fields automatically.
✅Copy Last Week saves time for staff with consistent schedules.
