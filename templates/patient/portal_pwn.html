How Your Patient Portal Works
PWN Health — Genetic Counseling Platform
What this portal does
🧬View your genetic test results and lab reports as soon as they're available.
📋Manage your consultations — schedule new sessions, join upcoming calls, and review history.
💬Send and receive secure messages with your genetic counselor between sessions.
⚠️When a result requires attention, you'll see an alert on your dashboard with a direct action to schedule a review consult.
Consult types
📹 Video Call Scheduled only
A live, face-to-face video session with your genetic counselor. Recommended for initial result reviews and detailed discussions. Must be scheduled in advance — we confirm within 1 business day.
📞 Phone Call Scheduled or On Demand
Your counselor calls your number on file. Choose a scheduled time or request an on-demand call — estimated wait 10–20 minutes.
💬 Async Message On Demand or Preferred Window
Submit your questions now and receive a detailed written response in your secure inbox. Response guaranteed within 72 hours.
Scheduling a consult — step by step
1
Select your topics — Tell your counselor what you want covered. Your abnormal results are pre-populated; add family history, risk options, or anything else.
2
Choose consult type & delivery — Pick Video (scheduled), Phone (scheduled or on demand), or Async (send now or preferred window).
3
Confirm — Review your selections. A unique request ID is generated and a confirmation is sent to your email. Your topics are shared with your counselor before the session.
On Demand vs. Scheduled
⚡On Demand (Phone) — Submit now; the next available counselor calls you within 10–20 minutes. No appointment needed.
⚡On Demand (Async) — Submit your questions immediately; receive a written response within 72 hours, any time of day.
📅Scheduled — Choose your preferred date and time window. We confirm within 1 business day and send a calendar invite.
After your consult
📄A session summary is added to your Consultation History within 1–2 business days.
🔬Any follow-up lab orders or referrals will appear in your Lab Results section.
📬If further action is needed, your counselor will message you directly through the secure portal.
