4 Special Case — Adverse Effect (All Consult Types)
🚨 Adverse Effect: If a patient reports an adverse effect at any point — during a consult, via a message, or independently — the report is immediately escalated as P0 Critical regardless of the configured consult type or release mode. Provider Queue and RN Queue are both notified simultaneously. Standard SLA and queue routing rules are bypassed entirely. A 911 emergency disclaimer is shown to the patient at the point of adverse effect selection. See UC2b for the detailed adverse effect escalation path.
