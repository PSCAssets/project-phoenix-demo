🔀 Age check at account creation — is patient under 18?
Path 1 — Product-first entry (arrived from website with care product selected): System validates DOB against that product's self-consent config. If self-consent eligible → minor creates independent account and proceeds. If not eligible → guardian flow initiated.

Path 2 — Direct entry (no care product context): System detects age under 18 from DOB and displays a prompt: "You appear to be under 18. Please select a care program that allows independent access for your age, or have your guardian set up your account." Patient must choose a path — cannot proceed without selecting one. No minor account created without either a guardian association or a verified self-consent eligible product.
↓
Guardian Flow (standard path for minors)
Guardian creates their OWN account first, then adds the minor as a dependent from Account Settings. This follows the industry-standard model (Epic MyChart, One Medical). Guardian is the account initiator — minor is never the initiating party. Once linked, guardian manages the minor's portal including security settings. Guardian receives account switcher to toggle between their own care and the dependent's care view.
↓
Step 7
Enters the portal for the first time
Full portal access granted immediately. Getting Started checklist appears on dashboard. For guardian-managed accounts: guardian logs in with their own credentials and switches to the minor's care view.
↓
Step 8
Selects or is enrolled in care program
Chooses a care program or is auto-enrolled via referral. For minors: care product age rules validated at enrollment. If program allows self-consent for minor's age range, minor proceeds independently. Otherwise guardian manages enrollment.
↓
🔀 Minor self-consent check at enrollment
Does this care product allow self-consent for the patient's age range? If yes (e.g., STI testing allows 14+) → minor proceeds independently for this product. If no → guardian manages enrollment, intake, and health profile until minor turns 18 or reaches care product self-consent age, at which point they receive an invitation to create their own independent account.
↓
Step 9
Signs program-specific informed consent
Separate consent document per enrolled care program. Required before intake begins.
↓
Step 10
Sets notification preferences
Prompted to configure email and/or SMS notifications. Editable later in Account Settings.
↓
Step 11
Completes required Health Profile sections
Required sections driven by care product config (e.g., A1C requires Medications, Allergies, Conditions, Measurements). Optional sections accessible but not required.
↓
Step 12
Completes intake questionnaire
Program-specific questions configured per care product in Admin Portal. Covers clinical history, symptoms, goals.
↓
🔀 Lab test required for this program?
If yes: patient receives lab order and collection instructions via portal + notification. If no: intake complete, consult routed directly.
↓
✅ Intake Complete — Awaiting Provider
Consult enters provider queue. Patient sees "Pending Review" status on dashboard.

Validates required identity fields
Checks all required fields populated. Validates email format, DOB format, address fields.
↓
Generates 6-digit OTP
Dispatches OTP via selected channel (email or SMS). OTP expires in 10 minutes.
↓
🔀 OTP valid?
If invalid or expired: resend available after 60-second cooldown. Max 3 attempts before lockout.
↓
Creates patient account record
Stores identity, credentials, 2FA config. Assigns unique patient ID. Logs account creation event.
↓
Serves legal documents (versioned)
Retrieves current versions of ToS, HIPAA NPP, Telehealth Consent for patient's state. Records acknowledgement with version, timestamp, patient ID.
↓
Loads care product configuration
Reads visit types, required health profile sections, intake questions, lab requirements, SLA rules for selected program and patient's state.
↓
Evaluates age at DOB entry — triggers appropriate path
System detects patient age in real time from DOB field. If under 18 AND a care product is known (product-first entry): validates against that product's self-consent config immediately. If under 18 AND no product context (direct entry): displays the two-path prompt — patient cannot proceed without selecting "Browse Eligible Programs" or "Have My Guardian Set Up My Account." No minor account created without guardian association or verified self-consent product.
↓
Serves program-specific informed consent
Generates consent document for enrolled program. Records acknowledgement.
↓
Generates Getting Started checklist
Populates checklist steps based on care product required fields and intake requirements. Tracks completion in real time.
↓
Validates health profile completeness
Checks required sections against care product config. Blocks intake submission if required sections incomplete. Displays prompts for missing fields.
↓
Validates intake questionnaire
Checks all required questions answered. Validates response formats. Detects clinical flags (e.g., high-risk responses that require escalation).

🔀 Lab required?
If yes: creates lab order in Phoenix, syncs to Athena, sends collection instructions via portal + Notification Engine. If no: routes to queue directly.

Routes consult to provider queue

↓
✅ Consult in Provider Queue
SLA clock starts. Provider assigned per queue rules. See UC2 for provider response flow.

📋 Guardian Flow (Industry Standard — Epic/One Medical model): Guardian creates their OWN account first, then adds the minor as a dependent. Minor is never the account initiator in the standard flow. Two entry paths exist for minors: (1) Product-first: minor arrives with a care product selected — self-consent validated at account creation. (2) Direct entry: system detects minor age and shows a prompt — "Browse Eligible Programs" or "Have My Guardian Set Up My Account." No minor account is created without either a guardian association or a verified self-consent eligible product. When minor turns 18 (or reaches care product self-consent age), they receive an invitation to create their own independent account and guardian access is revoked upon activation.
