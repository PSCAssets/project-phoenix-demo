Queue & Triage
System routing
P1 — Critical
Adverse effect, critical lab, escalation received. Auto-alerts sent. Immediate provider attention required.
P2 — Urgent
SLA < 4 hours. Time-sensitive clinical question or active symptom concern.
P3 — Semi-Urgent
SLA < 24 hours. Refill request, lab review, non-acute consult.
P4 — Non-Urgent
Standard async consult with no urgency flags. Default priority for most patient-initiated contacts. Majority of volume.
