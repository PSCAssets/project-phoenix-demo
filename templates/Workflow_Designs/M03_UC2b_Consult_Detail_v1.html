B Detailed Flow — Patient Login through Response Delivery
👤 Patient
Step 1
Logs into patient portal
Enters credentials. 2FA triggered on new device. Device remembered for 30 days after verification.
↓
Step 2
Views dashboard — reviews program status and action items
Sees program cards, action banner, Getting Started checklist status, recent lab results, and upcoming appointments.
↓
Step 3
Selects care program to initiate consult for
Multi-program patients select which program the consult is for. Single-program patients proceed directly.
↓
🔀 What type of consult is available?
Options displayed are driven by care product config per state. Patient sees only available consult types.
↓
Step 4A — Async
Completes async questionnaire
Fills in program-specific questions about symptoms, medications, goals, and any changes since last consult.
↓
Step 4B — Video / Telephonic
Selects appointment date and time
Views available provider slots. Selects preferred date and time. Confirms phone number (telephonic) or video access (video).
↓
Step 5
Reviews and submits consult (or confirms appointment)
Reviews summary before submitting. Confirmation screen shows expected response SLA (async) or appointment details (video/phone).
↓
✅ Consult submitted / Appointment confirmed
Patient sees "Pending Review" status on dashboard. Appointment appears in Upcoming tab.
↓
Step 9 — Video
Joins video visit at appointment time
Clicks "Join" from Appointments tab or email link. Enters waiting room. Provider admits when ready. Full video session in portal.
↓
Step 10
Receives notification of provider response
In-app, email, or SMS notification that response is available (async) or visit summary has been posted (video/phone).
↓
Step 11
Reviews provider response or visit summary
Reads treatment response, prescription details, recommended follow-up. Can send a follow-up message directly from the consult view.
↓
🔀 Does patient need a follow-up?
If yes: patient sends a follow-up message or books a new consult. If no: consult marked complete on dashboard.
↓
✅ Consult Complete
Moves to History in Appointments tab. Activity logged in patient Activity History.
⚙️ System (Auto)
Verifies 2FA on new device login
Checks if device is remembered. If new device, triggers OTP flow. On success, creates 30-day device session.
↓
Loads dashboard — dynamic state
Fetches all program statuses, open action items, unread messages, pending lab results, upcoming appointments. Renders action banner if items exist.
↓
Reads care product config per state
Determines available consult types for the patient's enrolled program and state. Only displays valid options.
↓
Loads program-specific questionnaire (async)
Retrieves intake questionnaire configured for the care product. Applies branching logic based on prior responses. Pre-fills known values from patient record.
↓
Shows available appointment slots (video / telephonic)
Fetches provider calendar availability. Displays open slots. Blocks unavailable times. Handles timezone conversion.
↓
Validates and submits consult
Validates all required questionnaire fields answered. Creates consult record. Logs submission event in audit trail. Sends confirmation to patient.
↓
SLA Engine assigns priority and routes to queue
Evaluates consult against SLA rules. Assigns P1–P4 priority. Routes to appropriate provider queue (MD, RN, or care team per consult type). Starts SLA clock.
↓
Sends appointment confirmation notifications
Patient: appointment confirmation with date, time, join instructions. Provider: new consult alert in queue. Both via configured notification channels.
↓
Monitors SLA clock — escalates if breached
Tracks time against SLA target. At 75% of SLA window: sends reminder to provider. At breach: escalates to admin and upgrades queue priority.
↓
Hosts video session (video consults)
Manages waiting room, patient admission, video stream. Maintains HIPAA-compliant encrypted connection. Records session duration for billing.
↓
Publishes response to patient portal
Makes provider response / visit summary visible in patient portal. Updates consult status to "Response available." Triggers patient notification.
↓
Delivers notification to patient
In-app notification + email or SMS per patient preferences. Notification links directly to the response in the portal.
↓
Closes consult — logs to audit trail
Updates consult status. Records completion timestamp. Logs all events to Audit Log (M12). Updates activity history in patient portal.
🩺 Provider / Care Team
Step 6
Receives consult in provider queue
New consult appears in queue with priority badge, patient name, program, consult type, and SLA timer. Alert sent via provider portal.
↓
Step 7
Opens patient chart and reviews consult
Reviews patient health profile, medication list, allergies, prior consult history, questionnaire responses (async), or prepares for live session (video/phone).
↓
🔀 Is this an adverse effect or urgent clinical issue?
If yes: escalates immediately — flags P1, notifies admin, responds within urgent SLA. If no: proceeds with standard response workflow.
↓
🚨 Urgent path: P1 escalation
Marks consult P1 Critical. Contacts patient immediately. Documents adverse event. May involve RN queue for clinical coordination.
↓
Step 8 — Async
Reviews questionnaire responses, writes treatment plan
Documents clinical assessment, recommendations, and treatment plan in chart. May update medications, place lab orders, or refer to specialist.
↓
Step 8 — Video / Phone
Conducts live consultation with patient
Communicates directly with patient. Takes notes during session. Uses side panel (video) for chart access, chat, patient info.
↓
Step 8 — Optional
Places lab order or prescription (if applicable)
Lab order synced to Athena. Prescription sent to patient's preferred pharmacy. Both appear in patient portal immediately.
↓
Step 9
Publishes response / visit summary
Submits treatment response (async) or visit summary (video/phone) to patient portal. Action logged in audit trail. Patient notification triggered.
↓
✅ Provider Workflow Complete
Consult moves to completed state. Chart updated. All events logged. SLA clock stopped.
📊 SLA Engine (M06)
Priority assignment rules
P0 — Immediate: Adverse effect, critical lab value
P1 — Urgent: Escalated clinical concern
P2 — Semi-urgent: New consult, active program
P3 — Standard: Routine follow-up, non-clinical
↓
SLA clock starts at submission
Timer begins when consult is submitted or appointment is confirmed. Configurable per care product and consult type.
↓
75% threshold — provider reminder
When 75% of SLA window has elapsed without provider action, a reminder notification is sent to the provider.
↓
SLA breach — escalation triggered
Queue priority upgraded. Admin portal receives alert. Admin can manually re-assign to available provider.
↓
SLA clock stops on response
Timer stops when provider publishes response or visit summary. Elapsed time recorded for reporting and compliance.
↓
⚠️ SLA values are configurable per care product in Admin Portal. Default SLA values per consult type and message type must be defined as part of the care product configuration matrix — currently an open item requiring cross-functional working session.
Adverse effect: bypass SLA entirely
Adverse effect reports skip standard SLA queue. Immediately escalated as P0. Provider and admin notified. RN queue also alerted per clinical protocol.
🔗 Module Dependencies: This flow touches M03 (Patient Portal), M05 (Async Consultation), M06 (SLA Engine), M02 (Provider Portal queue), M08 (Notifications), M10 (Lab order sync to Athena), M11 (Billing on visit completion), M12 (Audit Log).
📋 State Rules: Consult type availability (async/video/telephonic) is entirely driven by care product + state configuration in Admin Portal. Some states require video for initial visits. Some programs do not permit async at all. Configuration matrix must be fully defined before scheduling and booking can be built.
🚨 Adverse Effect Path: If patient sends an Adverse Effect message type at any point (not just during a formal consult), the same P0 escalation path fires immediately — bypassing the standard consult queue entirely. This is non-configurable and always active.
