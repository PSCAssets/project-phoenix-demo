✅ Async is the primary care model. No scheduling required — patients submit when ready, providers respond from the Master Patient Queue. Care product + state configuration in Admin Portal (M04) determines whether async is available for each program.
⚠️ Async response SLA: Not defined in V1.0. Response time is tracked via operational reporting. SLA values will be defined in V2.0 based on data collected in V1.0. SLA Engine (M06) will enforce SLA rules once defined.
A Async Consultation — Full Flow
👤 Patient
Step 1
Logs into patient portal
2FA verified. Dashboard shows program status and available consult options for enrolled programs.
↓
Step 2
Selects care program and initiates async consult
Clicks "Start Async Consultation" from dashboard program card or Appointments section. Multi-program patients select program first.
↓
Step 3
Completes structured clinical questionnaire
Fills in program-specific intake questions: symptoms, medications, goals, changes since last consult. Progress saved automatically. Can save and return later.
↓
Step 4
Reviews and submits consult
Reviews answers before submitting. Confirmation screen shown on submit. Portal status updates to "Pending Review."
↓
✅ Consult Submitted
Patient sees "Pending Review" status in Consultations section. Values hidden if lab result release mode is provider-reviewed.
Step 8
Receives response-ready notification
Notification received via in-app, email, or SMS per patient preferences. "Your provider has responded to your consultation."
↓
Step 9
Views provider clinical response
Opens structured response: Clinical Assessment, Treatment Plan, individual recommendation items (medication, diet, lab orders, follow-up), and provider note.
↓
🔀 Does patient need follow-up?
If yes: sends a follow-up message via Messages section referencing the consult. If no: consult marked Complete. Dashboard updates.
↓
✅ Consult Complete
Moves to History. Activity logged. Next check-in is notification-prompted (system sends reminder) or patient-initiated anytime from dashboard.
⚙️ System (Auto)
Loads care product questionnaire
Retrieves program-specific intake questionnaire from Admin Portal config. Applies branching logic. Pre-fills known values from patient health profile.
↓
Auto-saves progress
Saves patient answers in real time. Patient can leave and return without losing work. Draft state maintained until submission.
↓
Validates questionnaire on submit
Checks all required questions answered. Validates response formats. Detects clinical flags (high-risk responses). Creates consult record with unique consult ID.
↓
🔷 M06 SLA Engine — Priority Assignment
SLA Engine evaluates consult against program rules. Assigns P1–P4 priority. Routes to Master Patient Queue filtered by provider state and program assignments. SLA clock starts.
↓
Consult appears in Master Patient Queue
Visible to providers assigned to this program and state. Provider pulls consult from queue — not auto-assigned. Queue shows priority badge, wait time, patient program.
Response published to patient portal
Provider response made visible in patient Consultations section. Consult status updates from "Pending Review" to "Response Ready."
↓
🔷 M06 — SLA Clock Stops
SLA timer stopped on provider response. Elapsed response time recorded for operational reporting. V2.0: SLA compliance evaluated against defined targets.
↓
Consult status updated to Complete
On patient review and optional follow-up. Activity logged to patient Activity History and Audit Log (M12).
🔷 M06 — Next Check-in Notification Trigger
Based on care product config, SLA/Workflow Engine sends a proactive notification to patient when the next check-in is due: "It's time for your [Program] check-in. Start your consultation when ready." Patient can also initiate anytime from dashboard.
🩺 Provider
Step 5
Sees consult in Master Patient Queue
Consult appears in provider's filtered queue view. Provider sees only patients in their assigned states and programs. Priority badge, wait time, and program shown.
↓
Step 6
Selects and opens patient chart
Provider pulls consult from queue and opens patient chart. Reviews: health profile, medication list, prior consult history, and submitted questionnaire responses.
↓
🔀 Clinical flags in questionnaire?
If adverse effect or urgent clinical concern identified: escalates to P0/P1 and notifies RN queue. If standard: continues with normal response workflow.
↓
Step 7
Writes clinical response and treatment plan
Documents: clinical assessment, treatment recommendations (medication, diet, lifestyle), lab orders if needed, next check-in timeline. Adds optional personal note to patient.
↓
Publishes response to patient portal
Submits response. System publishes to patient portal and triggers patient notification. Consult moves to Completed in provider queue.
🔔 Notifications & Automation
📬 Submission confirmation to patient
Immediate: "Your consultation has been submitted. Your provider will review and respond." Sent via in-app + email/SMS per preferences.
🔷 M06 — SLA Threshold Reminders
At 75% of SLA window: Reminder notification to provider — "Pending response: [Patient Name] — [Program]." Queue item highlighted.

At SLA breach: Escalation alert to admin. Queue item flagged as Breached. Admin can reassign to available provider.
📬 Response-ready notification to patient
Immediate on provider publish: "Your provider has responded to your [Program] consultation. View your response in the portal." Sent via in-app + email/SMS.
🔷 M06 — Next Check-in Trigger
Based on care product configuration: system sends proactive notification when next check-in is due. "It's time for your [Program] check-in. Tap to start your consultation." Timing configured per product.
🔷 M06 SLA & Workflow Engine items flagged above are automation requirements for the upcoming M06 build: priority assignment at submission, SLA clock start/stop, 75% threshold reminders, breach escalation, and next check-in notification triggers. These must be captured in M06 BRD.
🔗 Related: Async questionnaire content lives in M04 Admin Portal (Intake Configuration). Lab orders placed in response flow through M10 (Athena sync). Notifications delivered by M08 (Notification Engine). Full audit trail in M12.
