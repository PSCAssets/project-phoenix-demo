New Async Consultation — Standard Flow

May 2026 · Everlywell Confidential
NEW Lab result upload gate — for products where this feature is enabled, patients may upload an existing lab result (e.g., A1C from the past 6 months) to satisfy the lab requirement before proceeding to queue entry. The accepted timeframe is a configurable setting per product. This gate is optional and product-scoped — not applied globally.
KEY Provider ownership model — a patient is not assigned to a provider until the provider takes the patient from the queue or an admin directly assigns them. The provider sees a consult notification in their queue, not the patient. The welcome message is sent automatically by the system at the moment provider takes ownership.
NOTE Adverse effect routing — if patient selects "Adverse Effect" as the request type, the consult routes simultaneously to Provider Queue + RN/MA/Care Team Queue + Admin alert. This is covered in detail in a separate use case: UC3 — Adverse Effect Escalation Flow.
