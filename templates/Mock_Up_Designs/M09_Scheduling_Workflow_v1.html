1 Four Availability Types — Provider Calendar
Scheduled Available
Open for all scheduled consults
Provider is available for scheduled Video and Telephonic appointments booked by patients or staff. Queue patients also welcome.
✓ Bookable by patients + staff
Queue Available
Working queue — no scheduled bookings
Provider plans to pull patients from the Master Queue. Scheduled appointments not bookable without provider approval notification.
⚠ Requires provider approval to book
Both
Scheduled + Queue simultaneously
Provider is available for both scheduled appointments AND pulling queue patients. Maximum flexibility and coverage.
✓ Fully bookable + queue active
Not Available
Vacation, personal time, out of office
Provider is blocked out completely. Hidden from all patient and staff booking views. No queue assignments during this time.
✗ Hidden from all booking views

🔷 M06 Rules Engine checks at every booking: Is this consult type enabled for patient state? Is provider licensed in patient state? Is provider assigned to this care product?
